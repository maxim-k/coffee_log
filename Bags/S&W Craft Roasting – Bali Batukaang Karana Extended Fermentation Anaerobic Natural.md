### Brewmaster J's Notes and Tips

S&W Bali Ext Fermentation Anaerobic Natural

I got lots of candied mixed berry in a relatively clean cup. I say relatively because it definitely has those anaerobic natural traits also found in the Costa Rica anaerobic naturals previously featured at S&W. If you push this, it will get very funky while lowered extraction will yield more candied berry cups. My best cups were deeply sweet, juicy candied berries coming from very low agitation methods and a coarser grind. I recommend something like the Gabi B, Melodrip, or Drip Assist for this.

- Grind 5.0 ZP6
- Temp 90C
- Brewer V60-01 + Gabi B
- Recipe 4 pour 15g/250g recipe 50/75/65/50 with a 45s bloom

### More about the producer and process

Karana has a well-appointed processing facility that includes optical color sorting for cherry with in-line flotation, plenty of raised bed drying space, and a crew of experienced coffee professionals who have handled everything from the soil to the final beverage. Starting from coffee harvested at 20-22 brix, the coffee is floated and washed, then placed in a fermentation tank after being inoculated with Pichia kudriavzeii, a yeast known to contribute complexity in flavor through its fermentation byproducts. The extended fermentation version of this coffee is macerated with Pichia yeast for 72 hours in the tank, until pH reaches 4.0, and then dried on the patio for 15-20 days, at which time the moisture content should reach 12%. The coffee is then hulled and rested in parchment for approximately 1 month before final hulling and export.
