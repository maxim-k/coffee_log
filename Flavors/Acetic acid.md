# Acetic acid

The sharp, sour smell of vinegar.

## References

- 1.0% acetic acid solution: aroma 2.5, flavor 3.0
- 2.0% acetic acid solution: aroma 3.0, flavor 4.5

Source: World Coffee Research, Sensory Lexicon 2.0 (2017). Intensity runs from 0 (none) to 15 (extremely intense).
