# Acrid

The sharp, bitter smell of something roasted too far.

## References

- Alf’s Natural Nutrition Red Wheat Cereal: aroma 3.0, flavor 3.0
- Wright’s Liquid Smoke Mesquite: aroma 9.5

Source: World Coffee Research, Sensory Lexicon 2.0 (2017). Intensity runs from 0 (none) to 15 (extremely intense).
