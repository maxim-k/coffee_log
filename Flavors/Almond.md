# Almond

Sweet, woody, buttery almond, sometimes with cherry or rose.

## References

- Le Nez du Café no. 27 “roasted almonds”: aroma 7.0

Source: World Coffee Research, Sensory Lexicon 2.0 (2017). Intensity runs from 0 (none) to 15 (extremely intense).
