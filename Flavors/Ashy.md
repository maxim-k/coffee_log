# Ashy

The dry, dirty smell of ash left after burning.

## References

- Gerkens 10/12 Midnight Black heavily alkalized cocoa powder: aroma 2.5, flavor 3.5
- Benzyl disulfide: aroma 4.0
- Paper ashes: aroma 4.0

Source: World Coffee Research, Sensory Lexicon 2.0 (2017). Intensity runs from 0 (none) to 15 (extremely intense).
