# Beany

The starchy, earthy smell of cooked beans.

## References

- Bush’s Pinto Beans (canned): aroma 7.0, flavor 7.5

Source: World Coffee Research, Sensory Lexicon 2.0 (2017). Intensity runs from 0 (none) to 15 (extremely intense).
