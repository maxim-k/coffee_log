# Bitter

The basic taste of caffeine.

## References

- 0.01% caffeine solution: flavor 2.0
- 0.02% caffeine solution: flavor 3.5
- 0.035 % caffeine solution: flavor 5.0
- 0.05% caffeine solution: flavor 6.5

Source: World Coffee Research, Sensory Lexicon 2.0 (2017). Intensity runs from 0 (none) to 15 (extremely intense).
