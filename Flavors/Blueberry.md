# Blueberry

Blueberry: dark, sweet, a little tart, dusty and flowery.

## References

- Oregon Fruit Products Blueberries in Light Syrup (canned): aroma 6.5, flavor 6.0

Source: World Coffee Research, Sensory Lexicon 2.0 (2017). Intensity runs from 0 (none) to 15 (extremely intense).
