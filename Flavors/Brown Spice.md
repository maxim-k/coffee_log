# Brown Spice

The sweet smell of baking spices such as cinnamon, clove, nutmeg and allspice.

## References

- Private Selection Cinnamon Sticks: aroma 3.0
- Private Selection Nutmeg (whole) and Private Selection Clove (whole): aroma 7.0
- Ground cinnamon, allspice, nutmeg, and clove mixture: aroma 10.5

Source: World Coffee Research, Sensory Lexicon 2.0 (2017). Intensity runs from 0 (none) to 15 (extremely intense).
