# Burnt

The dark, bitter smell of something overcooked.

## References

- Benzyl disulfide: aroma 4.5
- Raw peanuts, over-roasted/burnt: flavor 7.5
- Alf’s Natural Nutrition Red Wheat Cereal: aroma 8.0, flavor 3.0

Source: World Coffee Research, Sensory Lexicon 2.0 (2017). Intensity runs from 0 (none) to 15 (extremely intense).
