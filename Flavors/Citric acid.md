# Citric acid

A clean, mild sourness with a hint of citrus and some dryness.

## References

- 0.025 % citric acid solution: aroma 0.0, flavor 2.5
- 0.05 % citric acid solution: aroma 0.0, flavor 3.5

Source: World Coffee Research, Sensory Lexicon 2.0 (2017). Intensity runs from 0 (none) to 15 (extremely intense).
