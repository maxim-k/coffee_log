# Citrus Fruit

The tart, peely, slightly sweet and flowery smell of citrus fruit.

## References

- Peels of lemon and lime: aroma 4.5
- Grapefruit peel: aroma 7.5
- Five Alive Citrus (frozen concentrate): flavor 6.5

Source: World Coffee Research, Sensory Lexicon 2.0 (2017). Intensity runs from 0 (none) to 15 (extremely intense).
