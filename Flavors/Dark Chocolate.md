# Dark Chocolate

Intense cocoa, more bitter and drying, sometimes spicy or burnt.

## References

- Lindt Excellence 90% Cocoa Supreme Dark chocolate bar: aroma 6.0, flavor 11.0
- Dove Promises Silky Smooth Dark Chocolate (individually wrapped bite-sized bars): flavor 8.5

Source: World Coffee Research, Sensory Lexicon 2.0 (2017). Intensity runs from 0 (none) to 15 (extremely intense).
