# Dark Green

The heavy smell of cooked greens such as spinach or green beans.

- Del Monte Leaf Spinach (canned, liquid of): aroma 7.0, flavor 6.0

Source: World Coffee Research, Sensory Lexicon 2.0 (2017). Intensity runs from 0 (none) to 15 (extremely intense).
