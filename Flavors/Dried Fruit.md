# Dried Fruit

The sweet, slightly browned smell of dark dried fruit such as raisins and prunes.

## References

- Sunsweet Amaz!n Prune Juice: aroma 3.0, flavor 4.5
- Mixture of Sun-Maid Raisins and Sun-Maid Prunes: aroma 5.0, flavor 6.0

Source: World Coffee Research, Sensory Lexicon 2.0 (2017). Intensity runs from 0 (none) to 15 (extremely intense).
