# Fermented

A sweet-sour, yeasty, boozy smell of fermented fruit, sugar or dough.

## References

- Guinness Extra Stout beer: aroma 5.0
- Fermented grass: aroma 7.0

Source: World Coffee Research, Sensory Lexicon 2.0 (2017). Intensity runs from 0 (none) to 15 (extremely intense).
