# Grain

The dusty, lightly sweet smell of cereal grains.

## References

- Mixture of General Mills Rice Chex, General Mills Wheaties and Quaker Quick Oats cereals: aroma 5.0, flavor 8.0

Source: World Coffee Research, Sensory Lexicon 2.0 (2017). Intensity runs from 0 (none) to 15 (extremely intense).
