# Hazelnut

Woody, sweet, slightly earthy roasted hazelnut.

## References

- Le Nez du Café no. 29 “roasted hazelnuts”: aroma 5.5
- McCormick Imitation Hazelnut Extract, in milk: flavor 3.5, flavor 6.0

Source: World Coffee Research, Sensory Lexicon 2.0 (2017). Intensity runs from 0 (none) to 15 (extremely intense).
