# Herb-like

The smell of green kitchen herbs such as bay, thyme and basil.

## References

- Mixture of McCormick Bay Leaves, McCormick Ground Thyme, and McCormick Basil Leaves: aroma 6.0, flavor 5.0

Source: World Coffee Research, Sensory Lexicon 2.0 (2017). Intensity runs from 0 (none) to 15 (extremely intense).
