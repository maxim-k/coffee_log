# Isovaleric acid

A sharp, sour, sweaty smell like Romano cheese.

## References

- 0.2 µl/l isovaleric acid solution: aroma 3.0, flavor 4.0

Source: World Coffee Research, Sensory Lexicon 2.0 (2017). Intensity runs from 0 (none) to 15 (extremely intense).
