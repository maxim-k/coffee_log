# Malic acid

A sharp, fruity sourness, like green apple, with some dryness.

## References

- 0.5 g/l malic acid solution: flavor 3.0
- 1.0 g/l malic acid solution: flavor 5.0

Source: World Coffee Research, Sensory Lexicon 2.0 (2017). Intensity runs from 0 (none) to 15 (extremely intense).
