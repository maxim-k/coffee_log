# Medicinal

The clean, antiseptic smell of iodine, rubbing alcohol and bandages.

## References

- Iodine: aroma 3.0
- Alcohol: aroma 5.0
- Le Nez du Café no. 35 “medicinal”: aroma 6.0
- Band-Aid Plastic Strips adhesive bandage: aroma 6.0

Source: World Coffee Research, Sensory Lexicon 2.0 (2017). Intensity runs from 0 (none) to 15 (extremely intense).
