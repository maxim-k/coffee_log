# Molasses

Dark, caramelized sweetness with a sharp edge.

## References

- Grandma’s Original Molasses, unsulphured: aroma 6.5, flavor 6.5

Source: World Coffee Research, Sensory Lexicon 2.0 (2017). Intensity runs from 0 (none) to 15 (extremely intense).
