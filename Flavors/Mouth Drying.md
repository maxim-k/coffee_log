# Mouth Drying

A drying, puckering feel on the tongue: astringency.

## References

- 0.05% alum solution: flavor 2.5
- 0.07% alum solution: flavor 3.5

Source: World Coffee Research, Sensory Lexicon 2.0 (2017). Intensity runs from 0 (none) to 15 (extremely intense).
