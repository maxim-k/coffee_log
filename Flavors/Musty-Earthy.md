# Musty-Earthy

The heavy smell of damp soil and rotting leaves.

## References

- Miracle-Gro Potting Mix soil: aroma 9.0
- Le Nez du Café no. 1 “earthy”: aroma 12.0

Source: World Coffee Research, Sensory Lexicon 2.0 (2017). Intensity runs from 0 (none) to 15 (extremely intense).
