# Nutty

The sweet, woody, oily smell of nuts and seeds, often a little bitter.

## References

- Le Nez du Café no. 29 “roasted hazelnuts”: aroma 7.5
- Mixture of Diamond Sliced Almonds and Diamond Shelled Walnuts: flavor 7.5

Source: World Coffee Research, Sensory Lexicon 2.0 (2017). Intensity runs from 0 (none) to 15 (extremely intense).
