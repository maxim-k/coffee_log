# Oily

The fatty film left in the mouth after swallowing.

## References

- Horizon Organic Low-Fat (1%) ultrapasteurized milk: flavor 3.0
- Kroger Half & Half: flavor 6.0

Source: World Coffee Research, Sensory Lexicon 2.0 (2017). Intensity runs from 0 (none) to 15 (extremely intense).
