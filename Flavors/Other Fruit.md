# Other Fruit

The light, sweet, sometimes green or tart smell of tree and vine fruit such as apple, pear, peach, grape or cherry.

## References

- Le Nez du Café n.17 “apple”: aroma 7.0

Source: World Coffee Research, Sensory Lexicon 2.0 (2017). Intensity runs from 0 (none) to 15 (extremely intense).
