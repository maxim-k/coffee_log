# Pomegranate

Pomegranate: sweet-tart and dark, sometimes earthy like beet, sometimes drying.

## References

- R.W. Knudsen Organic Just Pomegranate Juice: aroma 5.5, flavor 7.5

Source: World Coffee Research, Sensory Lexicon 2.0 (2017). Intensity runs from 0 (none) to 15 (extremely intense).
