# Pungent

A sharp sensation that hits the inside of the nose.

## References

- Majestic Mountain Sage Orange Essential Oil, Brazil: aroma 5.0

Source: World Coffee Research, Sensory Lexicon 2.0 (2017). Intensity runs from 0 (none) to 15 (extremely intense).
