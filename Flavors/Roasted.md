# Roasted

The dark, browned smell of food roasted at high heat, short of burnt.

## References

- Raw blanched peanuts, lightly roasted: flavor 2.5
- Raw blanched peanuts, medium-roasted: flavor 6.5
- Raw blanched peanuts, dark-roasted: flavor 9.5
- Raw blanched peanuts, over-roasted/burnt: flavor 15.0

Source: World Coffee Research, Sensory Lexicon 2.0 (2017). Intensity runs from 0 (none) to 15 (extremely intense).
