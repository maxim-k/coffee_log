# Smoky

The sharp smell of smoke from burning wood or leaves.

## References

- Benzyl disulfide: aroma 3.5
- Diamond Smoked Almonds: aroma 6.0, flavor 5.0
- Wood Ashes: aroma 5.0

Source: World Coffee Research, Sensory Lexicon 2.0 (2017). Intensity runs from 0 (none) to 15 (extremely intense).
