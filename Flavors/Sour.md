# Sour

The basic taste of acid, as in a weak citric acid solution.

## References

- 0.015% citric acid solution: flavor 1.5
- 0.05% citric acid solution: flavor 3.5

Source: World Coffee Research, Sensory Lexicon 2.0 (2017). Intensity runs from 0 (none) to 15 (extremely intense).
