# Strawberry

Strawberry: sweet, a little tart, flowery, often wine-like.

## References

- Dole Whole Strawberries All Natural: aroma 13.0, flavor 6.0

Source: World Coffee Research, Sensory Lexicon 2.0 (2017). Intensity runs from 0 (none) to 15 (extremely intense).
