# Thickness

How thick the coffee feels against the tongue.

## References

- 5% sucrose solution: flavor 2.0
- Campbell’s Tomato Juice: flavor 4.0

Source: World Coffee Research, Sensory Lexicon 2.0 (2017). Intensity runs from 0 (none) to 15 (extremely intense).
