# Tobacco

The browned, sweet, slightly sharp smell of cured tobacco.

## References

- Le Nez du Café no. 33 “pipe tobacco”: aroma 5.0
- Camel cigarettes (Turkish and Domestic blend): aroma 7.0
- Cigar tobacco: aroma 10.5
- Unscented pipe tobacco: aroma 10.5

Source: World Coffee Research, Sensory Lexicon 2.0 (2017). Intensity runs from 0 (none) to 15 (extremely intense).
