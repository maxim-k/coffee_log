# Vegetative

The sharp smell of green vegetables such as parsley or asparagus.

## References

- Cut Asparagus Spears (canned): flavor 6.0

Source: World Coffee Research, Sensory Lexicon 2.0 (2017). Intensity runs from 0 (none) to 15 (extremely intense).
