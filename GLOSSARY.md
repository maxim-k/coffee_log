# Coffee Log

A personal Obsidian vault that records the coffees I buy and the brews I make
from them, so I can see which coffees, roasters and brewing setups I like.

## Language

**Bag**:
One physical bag of coffee I bought, from one roast on one roast date. Roasts
of the same coffee vary, so the Bag, not the product, is the unit I rate and
brew from.
_Avoid_: Coffee (when meaning the thing I bought), purchase

**Roaster**:
The company that roasted and sold the coffee, such as SEY or Passenger.
_Avoid_: Producer, brand

**Producer**:
The person, farm or co-op that grew the coffee, as printed by the Roaster.
_Avoid_: Roaster, farmer

**Brew**:
One cup I made from a Bag, with its brewing parameters: dose, water
temperature, grind setting, Grinder, Brewer and total brew time.
_Avoid_: Cup, session, recipe

**Grinder**:
A specific coffee grinder I own; each Brew uses exactly one.
_Avoid_: Mill

**Brewer**:
The device a Brew is made with, such as a V60; it stands for the brewing
method.
_Avoid_: Dripper, method
