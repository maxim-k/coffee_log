# Coffee Log

A personal Obsidian vault that records the coffees I buy and the brews I make
from them, so I can see which coffees, roasters and brewing setups I like.

## Language

**Bag**:
One physical bag of coffee I bought, from one roast on one roast date. Roasts
of the same coffee vary, so the Bag, not the product, is the unit I rate and
brew from.
_Avoid_: Coffee (when meaning the thing I bought), purchase

**Rating**:
My score from 1 to 5, given to a Brew for that cup or to a Bag for the coffee
as a whole.
_Avoid_: Score, stars

**Roaster**:
The company that roasted and sold the coffee, such as SEY or Passenger.
_Avoid_: Producer, brand

**Producer**:
The person, farm or co-op that grew the coffee, as printed by the Roaster.
_Avoid_: Roaster, farmer

**Variety**:
A genetic line of the coffee plant, such as Gesha or Caturra, with its parent
varieties and the other names roasters print for it. A Bag names its
Varieties by their one canonical name.
_Avoid_: Varietal, cultivar

**Brew**:
One cup I made from a Bag, with its brewing parameters: dose, water
temperature, grind setting, Grinder, Brewer, Water and total brew time.
_Avoid_: Cup, session, recipe

**Grinder**:
A specific coffee grinder I own; each Brew uses exactly one.
_Avoid_: Mill

**Grind setting**:
A position on one Grinder, written as rotations-number-clicks, such as
`2-2-3` (two full rotations, then number 2, then three more Clicks). Total
Clicks from True zero follow from it and the Grinder.
_Avoid_: Grind size (a Grind setting means nothing without its Grinder)

**True zero**:
The Click position where a Grinder's burrs just touch, counted from the
Grinder's own 0 mark; negative when it sits before the mark (my A2's is −7).
_Avoid_: Zero point, burr touch (when meaning the dial's 0 mark)

**Click**:
The smallest step a Grinder's adjustment can move.
_Avoid_: Step, notch

**Burr gap**:
The real distance between a Grinder's burrs. It is the only measure that
compares across Grinders, and it can differ from how far the adjustment
moves the burr: on my A2 one Click moves the burr 18 µm but changes the Burr
gap by roughly 11 µm, by my own estimate.
_Avoid_: Grind size, burr travel

**Brewer**:
The device I make a Brew with, such as a Hario V60 or an Orea O1; it stands
for the brewing method.
_Avoid_: Dripper, method

**Water**:
A named water I brew with, such as NYC tap, distilled, or distilled with a
mineral recipe added; each Brew uses one.
_Avoid_: Water profile, recipe (a recipe is how a Water is made)
