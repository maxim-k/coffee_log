# Coffee Log

A personal Obsidian vault that records the coffees I buy and the brews I make
from them, so I can see which coffees, roasters and brewing setups I like.

## Language

**Brew**:
One cup I made, with its brewing parameters: dose, water temperature, grind
setting, grinder and total brew time.
_Avoid_: Cup, session, recipe

**Grinder**:
A specific coffee grinder I own; each Brew uses exactly one.
_Avoid_: Mill
