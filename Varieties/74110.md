# 74110

A selection from wild forest coffee in southwestern Ethiopia, which the Jimma Agricultural Research Center collected in 1974 and released for resistance to coffee berry disease.

Source: [Aerts et al., 2013](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3689350/).
