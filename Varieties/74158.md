# 74158

A selection from Ethiopian landraces by the Jimma Agricultural Research Center, released in 1978/79 for resistance to coffee berry disease.

Source: [Tirfe et al., 2015](https://library.sweetmarias.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/Quality-Performance-of-Some-Early-Released-Coffee-Varieties-at-Three-Locations-in-South-West-Ethiopia.pdf).
