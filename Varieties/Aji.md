# Aji

A variety that José Herman Salazar found at Finca La Guaca, Colombia. Sources disagree on its genetics, so Parent and Genetic group stay empty. A test by the lab RD2 Vision, reported by Cup of Excellence, found an Ethiopian landrace. Cenicafé's fingerprinting found it 80% similar to Red Bourbon.

Sources: [Cup of Excellence, 2021](https://farmdirectory.cupofexcellence.org/listing/la-guaca-colombia-2021); [Cenicafé, 2025](https://publicaciones.cenicafe.org/index.php/infanual/article/download/4883/5069/3799).
