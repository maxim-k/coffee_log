# Arara

A Brazilian variety from a natural cross of Obatã with a yellow-fruited Catuaí or Icatu; the source doesn't say which. Obatã places it in the Sarchimor group.

Source: [Bartelega et al., 2017](https://www.alice.cnptia.embrapa.br/alice/handle/doc/1080029).
