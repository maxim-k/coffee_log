# Castillo

A composite variety that Cenicafé released in 2005: a blend of advanced progenies of Caturra × Timor Hybrid. Castillo El Tambo is one of its regional composites, made from the progenies that did best at Cenicafé's El Tambo station in Cauca.

Source: [Posada-Suárez et al., 2006](https://biblioteca.cenicafe.org/jspui/bitstream/10778/390/1/avt0344.pdf).
