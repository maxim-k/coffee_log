# Catucai 785

A Brazilian selection from the Catucaí group, which comes from a probable natural cross of Icatu and Catuaí. WCR has no entry for it; the group follows WCR's entry for IPR 103, a cross of the same parents.

Source: [Pinto et al., 2012](https://coffeescience.ufla.br/index.php/Coffeescience/article/view/283).
