# Caturra

Lineage: Natural mutation of the Bourbon variety

Source: [[WCR_Coffee_Varieties_Catalog.pdf|World Coffee Research, Coffee Varieties Catalog]] (2025).

Some sellers print Dwarf Caturra. No source names a distinct variety by that name, and nobody has published a test of one, so the name stays an alias of Caturra.
