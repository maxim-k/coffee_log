# Caturron

A local variety from Huila, Colombia. The roaster Tim Wendelboe reports its own DNA test of the beans: a cross of Timor Hybrid with Bourbon or Caturra. The roaster names no lab and publishes no data, so Parent and Genetic group stay empty.

Source: [Tim Wendelboe, 2025](https://timwendelboe.no/products/finca-tamana-caturron).
