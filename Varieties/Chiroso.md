# Chiroso

A variety from Urrao, Antioquia, Colombia. Sources disagree on its genetics, so Parent and Genetic group stay empty. A genotyping study links it to an Ethiopian landrace, not to Caturra. Cenicafé's fingerprinting found it 80–97% similar to Cenicafé progenies of Caturra Rojo × Cioccie Tipo I.

Sources: [Chica-Acosta et al., 2024](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13562-024-00934-9); [Cenicafé, 2025](https://publicaciones.cenicafe.org/index.php/infanual/article/download/4883/5069/3799).
