# Devamachy

Named as a parent in the [[WCR_Coffee_Varieties_Catalog.pdf|World Coffee Research, Coffee Varieties Catalog]] (2025), which has no entry for it.

A spontaneous Robusta × Arabica hybrid found on the Devamachy estate in India, the source of the Sln.5 selections. Source: [Kumar and Mishra, 2014](https://saspublishers.com/article/9030/download).
