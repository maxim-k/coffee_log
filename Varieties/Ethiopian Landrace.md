# Ethiopian Landrace

The wild and garden coffees of Ethiopia. Roasters print them as Heirloom.

The catalog names Ethiopian landrace as one of the main types of Arabica. Source: [[WCR_Coffee_Varieties_Catalog.pdf|World Coffee Research, Coffee Varieties Catalog]] (2025).
