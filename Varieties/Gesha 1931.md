# Gesha 1931

A selection that Gesha Village made after 2011 in the wild Gori Gesha forest of Ethiopia, from the trees most like Panama's Gesha. The name doesn't mean seed from 1931, and the line differs from the Panama Gesha.

Source: [SEY Coffee](https://seycoffee.com/products/oma-plot).
