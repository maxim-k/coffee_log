# HR61

A variety from the garden of about 70 varieties at Hacienda El Roble, Santander, Colombia; its name is its number there. SEY reports a genetic test that found an Ethiopian landrace close to Moka, but names no lab or study, so Parent and Genetic group stay empty.

Source: [SEY Coffee, 2021](https://www.seycoffee.com/products/oswaldo-acevedo).
