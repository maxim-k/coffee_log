# Icatu

Named as a parent in the [[WCR_Coffee_Varieties_Catalog.pdf|World Coffee Research, Coffee Varieties Catalog]] (2025), which has no entry for it.

IAC's breeders describe Icatu as derived from C. arabica × C. canephora hybrids, carrying part of the C. canephora genome. Source: [Maluf et al., IAC](https://www.alice.cnptia.embrapa.br/alice/bitstream/doc/902631/1/Caracterizacaoagronomica.pdf).
