# Ombligon

A variety from Huila, Colombia, named for the bump on its fruit. Roasters call it an Ethiopian landrace or a mutation of several varieties, but nobody has published a genetic test, so Parent and Genetic group stay empty. WCR's catalog lists Ombligon as another name of Monte Claro, a Venezuelan variety, which is probably a different plant.

Source: [Perfect Daily Grind, 2024](https://perfectdailygrind.com/2024/04/papayo-variety-coffee-competitions/).
