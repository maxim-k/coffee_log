# Papayo

A variety grown in Huila, Colombia, whose origin Cenicafé lists as not established. Cenicafé's fingerprinting found it 80–97% similar to a progeny of Castillo, a Caturra × Timor Hybrid composite. Roasters call it an Ethiopian landrace but cite no study.

Source: [Cenicafé, 2025](https://publicaciones.cenicafe.org/index.php/infanual/article/download/4883/5069/3799).
