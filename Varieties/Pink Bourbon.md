# Pink Bourbon

A variety from Huila, Colombia. Sources disagree on its genetics, so Parent and Genetic group stay empty. Lab tests by DNA Analytica and RD2 Vision, reported by the importer Cafe Imports, found an Ethiopian landrace, not a Bourbon. Cenicafé's fingerprinting found it 84–92% similar to progenies of Variedad Colombia.

Sources: [Cafe Imports, 2023](https://www.cafeimports.com/north-america/blog/2023/09/26/pink-bourbon-cryptozoology-and-genetics-in-specialty-coffee/); [Cenicafé, 2025](https://publicaciones.cenicafe.org/index.php/infanual/article/download/4883/5069/3799).
