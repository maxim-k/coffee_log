# Rume Sudan

Named as a parent in the [[WCR_Coffee_Varieties_Catalog.pdf|World Coffee Research, Coffee Varieties Catalog]] (2025), which has no entry for it.

No group: a genetic study places wild Boma Plateau coffee from South Sudan in a cluster of its own, apart from the Ethiopian landraces, while the Rume Sudan accessions it tested group with cultivated coffees, so none of the catalog's groups clearly fits. Source: [Krishnan et al., 2021](https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fsufs.2021.761611).
