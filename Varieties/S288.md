# S288

Named as a parent in the [[WCR_Coffee_Varieties_Catalog.pdf|World Coffee Research, Coffee Varieties Catalog]] (2025), which has no entry for it.

An Arabica line that carries C. liberica fragments, with a leaf rust resistance gene among them. Source: [Prakash et al., 2004](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15241596/).
