# SL9

A selection that Scott Agricultural Laboratories made in Kenya in the 1930s. A marker study places it in an "Ethiopian Legacy" cluster with SL14, SL34 and K7, but WCR puts those varieties in different groups, so Genetic group stays empty. Only an unpublished test links SEY's SL9 from Peru, also sold as Gesha Inca, to the Kenyan selection, and SEY calls that match provisional.

Source: [Montagnon et al., 2022](https://doi.org/10.3390/agronomy12123203).
