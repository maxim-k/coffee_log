# Sidra

An Ecuadorian variety. The WCR Coffee Varieties Catalog has no entry for it, and no published genetic study places it: roasters relay unattributed genotyping that points to an Ethiopian landrace, while others report that farms' "Sidra" may be several different plants. Parent and genetic group stay empty until a credible source places it.
