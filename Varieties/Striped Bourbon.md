# Striped Bourbon

A field selection by farmers in Sotará, Cauca, Colombia, named for its red cherries with orange stripes. It is often called a mutation of Red Bourbon, but nobody has published a genetic test, so Parent and Genetic group stay empty.

Source: [Intelligentsia, 2024](https://www.intelligentsia.com/blogs/stories/colombia-striped-red-bourbon).
