# Tabi

A tall, rust-resistant variety that Cenicafé released in 2002: a blend of progenies from crosses of Typica and Bourbon with Timor Hybrid 1343. Tabi means "good" in the Guambiano language.

Source: [Moreno-Ruiz, 2002](https://web.archive.org/web/2020/https://www.cenicafe.org/es/publications/arc053(02)132-143.pdf).
