# Timor Hybrid

Named as a parent in the [[WCR_Coffee_Varieties_Catalog.pdf|World Coffee Research, Coffee Varieties Catalog]] (2025), which has no entry for it.

It describes the Timor Hybrid as a natural cross of C. arabica and C. canephora from East Timor, the parent of most introgressed varieties.
