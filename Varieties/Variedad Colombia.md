# Variedad Colombia

A composite variety that Cenicafé released in 1982: a blend of advanced progenies of Caturra × Timor Hybrid 1343.

Source: [Alvarado-Alvarado et al., 2002](https://biblioteca.cenicafe.org/bitstream/10778/1011/1/arc053%2801%297-24.pdf).
