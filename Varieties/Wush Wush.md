# Wush Wush

A local coffee population from the Wushwush area of southwestern Ethiopia, grown in Colombia for about 30 years. It was never bred from certified seed and might hold many genotypes. Nobody has published a genetic test; its group follows from its Ethiopian origin.

Source: [Perfect Daily Grind, 2021](https://perfectdailygrind.com/2021/03/unlike-anything-i-had-ever-tasted-before-what-is-wush-wush-coffee/).
