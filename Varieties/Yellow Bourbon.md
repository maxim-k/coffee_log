# Yellow Bourbon

A yellow-fruited Bourbon from São Paulo, Brazil, that IAC selected from 1945. IAC gives two possible origins: a mutation of Red Bourbon, or a natural cross of Red Bourbon with Amarelo de Botucatu, a yellow Typica. Either way it belongs to the Bourbon-Typica group; the group takes the mutation reading, the one Yellow Bourbon is sold under, until a source settles which.

Source: [Carvalho et al., 1957](https://www.scielo.br/j/brag/a/WK6MsHmV37Dj9h6mPDtrsQD/abstract/?lang=en).
