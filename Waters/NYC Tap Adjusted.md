## Recipe
1. Place your clean, empty 150 ml bottle on a 0.01 g precision scale.
2. Add **0.83 g** of Epsom salt and **0.16 g** of calcium chloride.
3. Fill to the 150 ml mark with Brita-filtered tap water and shake vigorously until dissolved.
4. Pour **740 ml** of Brita-filtered tap water into your kettle.
5. Add **10 ml** of your booster solution.
6. Heat and brew.
