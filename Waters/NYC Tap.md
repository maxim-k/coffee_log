# NYC Tap

Published values: the citywide averages from NYC DEP's 2025 Drinking Water
Supply and Quality Report, which covers 2024.

Source: [NYC DEP 2025 Drinking Water Supply and Quality Report](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/dep/downloads/pdf/water/drinking-water/drinking-water-supply-quality-report/2025-drinking-water-supply-quality-report.pdf)

The values vary by neighborhood: areas that get Croton water, or a blend with
it, are harder than the citywide average.
