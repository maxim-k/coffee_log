# Roaster details without a property live in the Bag body

A Bag keeps as properties only the fields worth filtering on: what I bought,
and the coffee's origin, variety, process, and taste. Every other labeled
detail a roaster publishes, such as importer, harvest, prices, dates, or
fermentation, goes in the Details section of the note body as text copied
from the roaster. Few roasters publish these details, each words them
differently, and they would double the property list. Bases can't filter on
body text, which is acceptable until I normalize these details.
