# Bag properties hold harmonized values, the body keeps the roaster's text

Bag properties used to copy the roaster's text as written, so the same fact
came in many shapes: `2,000 m.a.s.l.` next to `1300 masl`, regions joined by
`<` or by commas, and tasting notes as a list on one bag and as two sentences
on the next. Those shapes made the properties hard to read side by side and
impossible to compare.

Bag properties now hold harmonized values: elevation as `<n> MASL`, regions
from the smallest area to the largest, tasting notes as a short
comma-separated list, the process by its name, and varieties as links to the
`Varieties/` dictionary. The note body keeps the roaster's text as written.
When harmonizing drops the roaster's words, not only their format, those
words go to Details as a `Label: as written` line, so the note keeps every
word the roaster published.
