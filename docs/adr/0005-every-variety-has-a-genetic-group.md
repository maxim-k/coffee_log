# Every Variety has a genetic group

`Genetic group` copied the WCR Coffee Varieties Catalog, so the 17 varieties
that the catalog names only as parents had none; Ethiopian Landrace and
Timor Hybrid were two of them. The site groups varieties by this property, and the
empty ones read as a group of their own.

A variety without a catalog entry now takes the catalog's group that a
source places it in, and its note cites that source: the catalog's own
introduction where it speaks of the variety, or else a genetic study or a
breeding institute. A variety no credible source places stays empty.

The catalog's Robusta groups (Congo, Guinea, Uganda) say nothing of the
species, so a Robusta's group reads `Robusta (<catalog group>)`, and
Robusta itself is `Robusta`. Nemaya, which the catalog places in Unknown,
becomes `Robusta (Unknown)`.
